# V4 pursuit and opponent-diversity repair

The earlier V2/V3 scripted win rates did not measure pursuit. Both policies
scored zero points in all 1,024 fixed-start, 20-second stationary-opponent
matches. The pursuing script brought itself into range of their attacks.
The [original diagnostic](afk-policy-20260915/finding.md) preserves that failure.

V4 adds GPU-side training opponents and randomized starts, extends the learner
horizon and discount, and provides a frozen older-policy mixture. Verified
weights-only continuation also fixes a pinned trainer path that accepted the
initial-checkpoint option without loading it for training. The selected policy
was trained through close-contact, pursuit, arbitrary-heading and mixed-opponent
stages. Canned movement, contact scoring and the 33-action interface are unchanged.

## Training throughput and deployment

The selected four-stage lineage contains 704,643,072 learner transitions over
316.612160921 training-loop seconds: **2,225,571.721 training SPS** combined.
This includes rollout and PPO. Total process time was 319.21 s. The final mixed
stage achieved 1,715,495.338 SPS with additional frozen-policy inference on all
512 rows; the first three stages achieved 2.717 to 2.741 million SPS.
Simulation, inference and PPO run on Spark's GPU in native C++/CUDA. No Python
or CPU physics is used in training. Browser rendering is separate.
See [stage measurements](semantic-fast-v4-training-20260915/summary.json).

The main compact viewer at `http://127.0.0.1:18769/` now selects this checkpoint,
with the human on orange and five-minute rounds. It exposes per-condition
results, exact checkpoint/inference identity, training statistics and the
measured league ranking. Previous policy and service files remain preserved.
Other viewers and the unrelated overlay service were left running.

The later 300-second fine-tune was rejected after measured regressions,
including losing direct head-to-head comparisons with the selected checkpoint.
Its training cost and transitions are recorded separately and excluded from
the selected policy's lineage and throughput figures.

These measurements establish a substantial repair of the stationary-target
failure. They do not establish universal wins, human superiority or authentic
REK parity. Sustained frontal alignment remains inconsistent. Genuine physical
knockdowns and full rigid-body dynamics are still absent from this reduced
candidate. Human evaluation remains necessary.
