Simulator and training interface

The native app reuses extracted G1 meshes, motion assets and motor-controller weights. Unity shaders, authoritative server state and full gameplay parity remain unverified.

The view uses the complete extracted arena and diagnostic lighting. Collision geometry is loaded separately by the native simulator.

Training clients can read /api/protocol, inspect /api/snapshot, pause, reset and step the same process. Manual play and explicit stepping are serialized. Session inputs, states and frames are recorded.
